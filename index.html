Marginalia

Marginalia is a place to keep the quotes and links you don't want to lose — a line from an article, a passage from a book, something a podcast said that stuck. Save it with a note on why it mattered, and every Saturday at 10am you'll get a short digest pulling out the themes across your week.

Your notes are private to your account and yours alone — append-only, so nothing you've saved can be edited or deleted out from under you.
