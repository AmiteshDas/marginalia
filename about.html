What Marginalia is for

Marginalia helps you keep track of two kinds of things: what already caught you, and what you still mean to get to.

Notes are for a line from something you've read, heard, or watched. Save it the moment it strikes you, with a quick line on why you've noted it down (if you'd like). Notes are append-only: once written, nothing here can be edited or deleted out from under you.

Shelf is for what's ahead of you. Add something the moment you hear about it, start it when you actually want to, and Marginalia tracks the momentum: fresh, warm, or cold, based on how recently you've touched it. Let something sit untouched for a month and it's asked about once, then quietly archived if you've moved on.

This Week pulls the themes out of your week's notes every Saturday at 10am, and flags anything on your shelf gone cold.

Latest update

Notes and Shelf now stay separate — something you add to read later won't show up as a note until you actually write one about it. You can connect a note back to the shelf item it came from, add a "Long-form Paper" source, and there's this About page.
